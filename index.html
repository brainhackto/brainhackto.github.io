Brainhack Toronto

We bring people together to share ideas, tools, and knowledge—and make brain research more open, collaborative, and reproducible.

BrainHack Toronto will be held here at SickKids Jan 20-22, 2027: Responsible, transparent, and reproducible AI for brain hacking

Ahead of the event, we encourage trainees and PIs to think about potential projects they could pitch for the event. It’s an exciting opportunity to engage with interdisciplinary teams across Neuronto networks and beyond.

Keep an eye out for upcoming registration and feel free to reach out with questions or opportunities to volunteer for the organizing committee via brainhacktoronto@gmail.com
